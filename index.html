Examination Mode Selection

Select the examination protocol appropriate for your imaging setup:

Regular Camera Examination

Macroscopic Analysis

Gross morphological analysis using a mobile phone or digital camera. Suitable for assessing gross hair length, curvature/wave pattern, macroscopic color, and shaft integrity.

  • Requires high-contrast clean background
  • Supports real-world calibration using a standard millimeter ruler
  • Results will be strictly designated under Regular Camera Examination

Microscopic Examination

High Magnification

Microscopic structural examination via an eyepiece camera adapter or photomicrograph upload. Suitable for examining cuticle scale pattern, medulla index/continuity, cortex pigmentation, root, and tip morphology.

  • Requires mounted specimen on clean glass slide with coverslip
  • Supports micrometer calibration (µm) with stage micrometer scale
  • Results will be strictly designated under Microscopic Examination
